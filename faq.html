Quick answers about The Daily Aux.

  • What is the goal of this website?
    To spread the word about music news and share our favorite works of music with our audience.
  • Do you sell concert tickets directly on this website?
    No, but we provide accurate information which allows our audience to know what artists they can find to buy tickets for.
  • How do you determine who makes the "Top Trending Artists" list?
    We stay up to date with the trends in the media as well as follow the Billboard Hot 100.
  • How do I subscribe to the weekly newsletter?
    Our Newsletter is not yet up and running, but stay tuned!
  • I found broken links or incorrect information. How do I report it?
    Visit the Contact page to send custom questions and suggestions directly to the team.
